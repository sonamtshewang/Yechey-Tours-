Festival calendar

Plan around a tshechu

    Punakha Drubchen and TshechuRe-enactment of a 17th-century battle, then masked dances.
  • Mar–Apr
    Paro TshechuEnds with the dawn unveiling of the giant thongdrel.
  • Sep–Oct
    Thimphu TshechuThe largest festival, held at Tashichho Dzong.
  • Nov
    Black-necked Crane FestivalPhobjikha's school children celebrate the cranes' return.
  • Nov

Festival dates follow the Bhutanese lunar calendar and change each year. We confirm exact dates when you enquire.

Visa and fees, step by step

  1. Choose your tour and dates with us.
  2. Send a clear passport scan and photo.
  3. We apply for your visa and pay the Sustainable Development Fee (SDF) on your behalf.
  4. You receive the visa clearance letter before you fly.
  5. Your visa is stamped on arrival at Paro or Phuentsholing.

Indian, Bangladeshi and Maldivian nationals pay a lower SDF and use a permit instead of a visa. SDF rates are set by the government; we quote the current rate with every booking.

About us

A local team, born in these valleys

We are a Bhutanese tour company run by people who grew up here. We plan every trip ourselves and travel with you on the ground.

From cultural tours and tshechu festivals to high-altitude treks, we shape each journey around your pace and interests. Our guides are licensed by the Department of Tourism, and our drivers, cooks and trekking crews are people we have worked with for years.

Bhutan's "high value, low volume" tourism means fewer crowds and more respect for local life. We keep our groups small so you meet people, not just see places.
